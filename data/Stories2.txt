Impactful Film for National Competition on "ODF Plus Model Village"
In a significant stride towards enhancing community awareness and engagement with sanitation initiatives, Megh Foundation’s dedicated volunteers have produced a compelling film for the National Film Competition, an initiative by the Ministry of Jal Shakti. The film, centred on the theme "ODF Plus Model Village," aims to highlight and promote the successful implementation of the ODF (Open Defecation Free) Plus model, which extends beyond basic sanitation to include improvements in hygiene, waste management, and overall community health. The ODF Plus Model: A Comprehensive Approach The ODF Plus model is an advanced framework established by the Ministry of Jal Shakti to build upon the success of achieving ODF status. It focuses on maintaining and improving sanitation standards by integrating additional components such as: Sustainable Waste Management: Proper disposal and management of solid and liquid waste. Improved Hygiene Practices: Promotion of handwashing, safe drinking water, and sanitation. Community Engagement: Active involvement of local communities in maintaining sanitation and hygiene standards.
Impact and Community Engagement The film produced by Megh Foundation’s volunteers serves as a powerful tool for raising awareness and driving positive change. Key impacts include. The film educates viewers about the ODF Plus model, emphasizing the importance of moving beyond basic sanitation to achieve comprehensive improvements in community health. By showcasing successful examples and real-life stories, the film inspires other communities to adopt similar practices and work towards achieving ODF Plus status. The film highlights the crucial role of community engagement in maintaining sanitation standards and improving overall quality of life. Film Production Rahul Rupavel and Dhrumil Jadav from Leolight Studio, passionate about social change and environmental sustainability, took on the challenge of creating a film that effectively showcases the ODF Plus model. The process included concept development, scripting, and storyboarding, focusing on real-life examples of the model’s success in villages. Filming involved capturing authentic footage in model villages, including interviews with community members, officials, and experts. The editing emphasized a clear, engaging narrative that highlighted the model’s benefits for sanitation and health. The completed film was promoted widely to raise awareness and submitted for consideration in a national competition.

Facilitation Of Government Job Candidates
In today’s competitive job market, securing a government job is a dream for many aspiring candidates. However, the path to this dream is often riddled with challenges, especially when it comes to the complex process of application submission. From filling out lengthy forms to understanding intricate eligibility criteria, many candidates struggle with the paperwork. Recognizing this issue, Megh Foundation has stepped in to support government job aspirants by providing free form filling knowledge and services. Their initiative has been gaining traction, offering invaluable help to those who may not have the resources or expertise to navigate this crucial stage of the job application process. Megh Foundation's hand-holding support system, which includes providing accommodation assistance for low-income students, is a vital step in ensuring that no one is left behind due to financial limitations. By collaborating with other organizations, Megh Foundation has taken a remarkable step forward by offering specialized training programs tailored to aspiring police candidates. These programs not only focus on physical fitness but also provide essential support to ensure that young candidates are fully prepared for the rigorous demands of police examinations. What Megh Foundation Offers Megh Foundation goes beyond just offering assistance with filling forms. They provide candidates with comprehensive knowledge about the entire application process. This includes educating aspirants on: - How to correctly fill out various government job application forms. - Understanding the necessary documentation. - The steps involved in submitting applications online or offline. In addition to knowledge sharing, the foundation also offers hands-on support with filling out forms. This service is especially beneficial for candidates who lack digital literacy or access to computers and internet services. Megh Foundation’s volunteers help candidates accurately complete the application forms, ensuring all necessary fields are filled out correctly. Workshops and Training Megh Foundation organizes workshops and training sessions, where experts and volunteers guide candidates’ step by step through the form filling process. These sessions also cover other vital aspects of the job application process, such as writing a compelling resume, preparing for interviews, and understanding exam patterns. These workshops are typically free of charge and open to all.
The Impact of Programs The Megh Foundation’s training programs have already made a significant impact on many aspiring police officers. Participants have reported improved physical fitness, increased confidence, and a stronger sense of purpose. The combination of rigorous physical training, proper nutrition, and personalized support has equipped them with the tools they need to excel in their police exams and, ultimately, in their careers in law enforcement. By investing in the future of these young individuals, the Megh Foundation is not only preparing them for the physical demands of police work but also contributing to the development of a dedicated and capable workforce. Their holistic approach ensures that candidates are not only fit but also inspired to pursue their dreams of serving their communities with integrity and commitment. This is where Megh Foundation’s initiative comes into play. By offering free form filling assistance, the foundation helps bridge this gap, ensuring that all aspiring candidates have an equal opportunity to apply for government jobs, regardless of their socioeconomic status.

Adequate nutrition is crucial for cognitive and physical development. By ensuring that children receive proper nourishment, the foundation hopes to enhance their academic performance and overall health, contributing to better long-term outcomes. The program seeks to bridge the gap between privileged and underprivileged children by providing a basic nutritional need that is often unmet in lower income settings. This initiative is a step toward reducing health disparities and promoting equal opportunities for all children.

Shubh Yatra, Swachh Yatra
In a significant step toward promoting cleanliness and community awareness, Megh Foundation recently organized the "Shubh Yatra, Swachh Yatra" program at Kalol Bus Station. Megh Foundation leveraged engaging and interactive methods to connect with the public and inspire action. Volunteers painted vivid murals on the walls of Kalol Bus Station, illustrating messages about hygiene, cleanliness, and civic responsibility. These artworks now stand as a visual reminder for everyone to keep their surroundings clean. Public Interaction and Talks: Megh Foundation’s representatives actively engaged with the public through one-on-one conversations and group discussions. These interactions served as an opportunity to educate people about the importance of cleanliness, proper waste disposal, and maintaining hygiene in public places. Public Reviews and Feedback: As part of the initiative, the Foundation also collected reviews and feedback from the public. This provided valuable insights into the challenges and perspectives of the community, helping shape future campaigns more effectively A Step Toward a Cleaner Future Megh Foundation’s dedication to raising awareness and promoting cleanliness reflects the organization’s broader vision for societal progress. By aligning with the Gujarat Government’s initiative, the Foundation not only enhanced the cleanliness of Kalol Bus Station but also instilled a sense of civic responsibility among the people.

Walking in His Footsteps — Megh Foundation Youth Visit Gandhi Ashram, Ahmedabad
There are places in the world where the air itself feels different — quieter, heavier with meaning, alive with the memory of greatness. Gandhi Ashram in Ahmedabad is one such place. And on a remarkable day, around 50 youth volunteers and youth leaders of Megh Foundation walked through its humble grounds, not just as visitors, but as eager learners ready to carry forward a legacy that changed the world. The visit was more than a field trip. It was a pilgrimage of purpose.As the group gathered at the banks of the Sabarmati river, the simplicity of the Ashram spoke before anyone else did. No grand architecture, no towering monuments — just modest rooms, a spinning wheel, and the quiet discipline of a life lived with absolute intention. For many of the young volunteers, it was their first time truly understanding what it means to live simply and lead powerfully.The day was centered around the core teachings of Mahatma Gandhi — a philosophy that feels as urgent today as it did a century ago. The youth engaged deeply with the concept of Swadeshi, Gandhi's powerful call to embrace what is local, what is our own. They learned how this simple idea — of choosing homegrown over imported, of taking pride in one's roots — was not just an economic movement, but a revolution of self-reliance, dignity, and identity. The message resonated strongly with the young leaders: true change begins close to home. Equally moving was the lesson on simple living. In a world that constantly pushes more — more consumption, more speed, more noise — Gandhi's life offered a radical counter-vision. The volunteers walked through the rooms where he slept, worked, prayed, and planned the freedom of a nation with nothing more than discipline, clarity, and conviction. It was a powerful reminder that impact does not require excess. The peaceful way of life that Gandhi championed — Ahimsa, non-violence, truth — was brought to life not through lectures, but through stories. Inspiring stories of how a quiet, soft-spoken man stood firm against an empire. Of how he walked 241 miles for salt and shook the foundations of colonial rule. Of how he believed, unshakeably, that every human being carries within them the capacity for courage, compassion, and change. For the youth leaders of Megh Foundation, these were not just history lessons. They were mirrors — reflecting back the values they already hold and the leaders they are becoming.The visit left its mark in the most beautiful way. Young volunteers who arrived curious left inspired. Youth leaders who came to learn left with a deeper sense of responsibility. Everyone carried something home — not in their bags, but in their hearts. Megh Foundation believes that the future belongs to those who are rooted in values and bold in vision. What better place to nurture that spirit than the very soil where Gandhi himself dreamed of a better India.As the group walked out of the Ashram, the river flowed on beside them — steady, purposeful, unhurried. Much like the philosophy they had just embraced.Be the change. Live simply. Choose Swadeshi. Keep inspiring.


A Moment of National Pride — Megh Foundation's "Community Friend" Initiative Honored at Rubaroo's Interfaith Youth Leadership Conference, Hyderabad
By Brijesh Solanki  
Some moments stop you in your tracks and remind you exactly why you do what you do. This was one of those moments.
We are deeply honored and humbled to share that our initiative "Community Friend" has been recognized at the National Conference of the Interfaith Youth Leadership Program, organized by Rubaroo, Hyderabad — a gathering of some of the most passionate, purpose-driven changemakers from across the country.
Standing in that room, surrounded by young leaders who have chosen dialogue over division, empathy over indifference, and action over silence, felt like coming home. Because that is exactly the spirit that Community Friend was born from.
What Community Friend Stands For
Community Friend is not just a program. It is a belief — a belief that the youth of today carry within them the power to build the India of tomorrow. An India where communities do not merely coexist, but truly understand each other. Where differences in faith, background, and identity are not walls that divide, but windows that open us to richer, deeper human connection.
Through youth leadership and interfaith dialogue, Community Friend has been working quietly and consistently to do exactly that — creating spaces where young people listen before they speak, question before they judge, and lead with their hearts as much as their minds.
The Recognition
Being honored at a national platform like Rubaroo's Interfaith Youth Leadership Conference is not just a milestone for our team — it is a validation of every young volunteer who showed up, every difficult conversation that was held with grace, every community space that was made a little more inclusive because someone chose to try.
This recognition belongs to every member of our team, every youth leader who believed in this vision, and every community that opened its doors and trusted us with their stories.
A Word of Gratitude
We extend our deepest thanks to Rubaroo, Hyderabad, for creating this extraordinary national platform — a space that elevates youth voices and reminds us that interfaith understanding is not idealism, it is a necessity. Their work in bringing together changemakers from across India is a gift to this generation and the ones that will follow.
To every fellow leader, organization, and individual we met at this conference — thank you for reminding us that we are not alone in this work. The movement is bigger than any one of us, and that is its greatest strength.
The Road Ahead
Recognition is a beautiful thing. But it is also a responsibility. This honor does not mark an arrival — it marks a deeper commitment. A commitment to build more bridges, reach more communities, train more youth leaders, and hold more conversations that matter.
We return from Hyderabad not just with an award, but with renewed energy, stronger connections, and an even clearer sense of purpose.
The world needs more spaces of mutual respect. More rooms where people of different faiths sit together not to debate, but to understand. More young people who see their neighbor's story as part of their own.
Community Friend will keep building those spaces — one relationship, one dialogue, one community at a time.
Together, let's continue creating a tomorrow worth living in.
— Brijesh Solanki, Megh Foundation
